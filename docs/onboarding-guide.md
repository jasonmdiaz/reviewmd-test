# Team onboarding guide

This guide covers a new teammate's first two weeks, and what to expect from the team in return.

## Before day one

- Accounts are created by IT.
- A laptop ships to your home address.
- Your manager sends a welcome email with the first week's schedule.
- You get a buddy: a teammate who answers the questions you would rather not ask in a channel.

## Week one

Spend the first week reading and pairing. Nobody expects you to ship code.

| Day | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Setup and introductions |
| Tuesday | Architecture overview |
| Wednesday | Pair with a teammate |
| Thursday | Pair with a teammate |
| Friday | Retro with your manager and your buddy |

## Week two

Pick a starter task labeled `good-first-issue`. Aim to open a pull request by Thursday, so review can finish before the weekend.

## Getting help

Ask in the team channel, or ask your buddy directly. Every question is welcome.

If you are blocked for more than an hour, say so. Waiting quietly costs more than asking.

```bash
# Check your setup
make doctor
```
